Step by step How to use Placard Pro 1 Upload your manifest Start by uploading a clear photo of the manifest or tally sheet. The scanner reads it in the background to figure out how many BOLs contain hazmat — so you only upload the ones that matter. 💡 Take a photo with your phone (JPG/PNG). PDFs cannot be scanned — use a photo instead. 2 Upload the hazmat BOLs Once the manifest is read, upload only the hazmat bills of lading it flagged. Each BOL is analyzed as its own item with its own UN number, weight, and packaging. 💡 Make sure weight, UN number, and hazard class are legible in the photo — these drive the placard result. 3 Run the analysis Press Analyze. Placard Pro reads every BOL, extracts the hazmat data verbatim, applies 49 CFR placard rules, checks bulk/mixed-load logic, and flags any missing fields on the documents. 💡 You'll see live status messages while it works. Results appear automatically when done. 4 Review required placards The report shows exactly which placards to display, the CFR rule behind each one, total weight per class, and whether DANGEROUS may substitute for individual class placards on a mixed load. 💡 Bulk items show their UN number placard separately. Compatibility warnings flag materials that must be segregated. 5 Check emergency guidance Tap any hazmat item to pull live ERG 2024 data — firefighting media, spill isolation distances, protective action zones, PPE, and first aid. Share or save the report for your records. 💡 Emergency contact numbers from your BOL are surfaced too, including CHEMTREC (1-800-424-9300). 6 Dispute, rescan, or save If a placard looks wrong, dispute it with a photo and reason — you get a free rescan credit and your fleet admin reviews it. Otherwise, save the analysis to your history for an audit trail. 💡 Every scan is stored to your Hazmat History so you can pull it up anytime.
